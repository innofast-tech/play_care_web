1. About this app

Play Care Gate is used by ticket checkers at the entrance of Play Care, a kids' indoor playground. Staff use it to check members in by scanning a QR code and to close visits when families leave.

The app is not for the public. Staff accounts are created by the Play Care administrator, so you cannot sign up in the app yourself.

2. Information the app handles

Your sign-in details

Your phone number and password, used to sign you in to the account the administrator made for you. If you choose "Keep me signed in", the app stays signed in on that phone.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan QR codes at the gate. CONFIRM The app does not save photos or video.

Member details shown at the gate

After a scan, the app shows the member's name, photo, plan and validity. It also shows the name, photo, gender and age of the guardians and children on that membership, so you can check who is entering.

Gate activity

Which sessions you started or closed, and when. These records are linked to your staff account.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and check what your account is allowed to do.
  • To show the right member and family details when you scan a QR code.
  • To record check-ins and check-outs, so visit times and extra charges are correct at the counter.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

4. Who we share it with

5. Children's information

The app is for staff, not for children. It does show the names, photos, gender and age of children on a membership, only so staff can confirm who is entering and leaving.

Staff must not photograph, copy or share this information.
